## What 1.0.0 means

1.0.0 is not "more features." It is the point where Athanor is:

- **trustworthy** — containment, recovery, observability, and a completed
  security audit hold under real use;
- **measured** — a mature, MCE-sensitive frozen benchmark and a concluded
  statistical study, not just directional anecdotes;
- **self-improving** — selection (technique, model, routing, escalation) is
  learned from grounded outcomes and the human anchor, reversibly;
- **pleasant** — a **first-class GUI** covers the daily loop, not a CLI-only
  workflow;
- **extensible** — skills, tools, and integrations are a platform, not a fixed
  set;
- **portable** — a signed install, a Core Pod, and a clean upgrade on Linux and
  macOS.

0.0.x is where the study runs; 1.0.0 is where its questions are answered and the
system is a daily driver.

## Pillars

| # | Pillar | Acceptance theme |

## Not 1.0.0 (keep the boundary)

- Multi-user / teams / cloud sync (Athanor is single-operator, local-first).
- Cloud-first inference (cloud may be a HITL-gated teacher, never the deployed
  model).
- Unbounded autonomy for external or irreversible actions.
- Unbounded self-modification.
- Every model/hardware combination.

## Acceptance themes (draft)

- A full day of real work, end to end, **without the CLI**.
- The frozen benchmark shows **no regression** across the major, and the
  study's claims reproduce on the frozen benchmark.
- The operator can **approve a learned change, roll it back**, and the system
  records both.
- A crash, power loss, or upgrade **loses no state**.
- A **fresh install on Linux and macOS** completes cleanly.
